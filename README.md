## Features

- **Stackable Geometry:** Engineered with an interior hollow profile so cones nest tightly inside each other for storage and transport, just like real-world traffic cones.
- **Print-in-Place:** 100% support-free design — prints upright with zero assembly or post-processing required.
- **RC & Scale Ready:** Pre-scaled models included for popular RC scales (1:8, 1:10, 1:12, 1:14, 1:16, 1:18, 1:24).
- **Fully Parametric Source CAD:** Includes Autodesk Fusion 360 (`.f3d`) and universal STEP (`.step`) files for custom sizing.

## Recommended Print Settings

- **Layer Height:** `0.10 mm` – `0.16 mm` (0.10 mm recommended for smooth overhangs and proper nesting clearance)
- **Walls / Perimeters:** `2` to `3`
- **Infill:** `15%` – `20%` (Grid, Gyroid, or Rectilinear)
- **Supports:** **None** (prints cleanly upright)
- **Bed Adhesion:** Brim recommended for smaller scales (e.g. 1:24 and 1:18) to ensure reliable bed contact
- **Materials:**
  - **PLA / PETG:** Great for rigid, clean diorama pieces and course markers
  - **TPU / Flexible:** Highly recommended for RC car racing courses — bendable, indestructible, and won't break on collision!
- **Color Accent:** Pause at height or use multi-material / multi-color to print white reflective safety stripes, or paint with a white paint pen.
